# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are academic conference organizers and chairs coordinating a complete conference lifecycle. Authors and reviewers are essential participants, but the public landing page primarily persuades organizers.

## Product Purpose

FresiCMT gives organizers one place to run conference workflows from manuscript submission through peer review, decisions, camera-ready collection, and registration. Success means replacing fragmented spreadsheets, inboxes, and disconnected tools with a precise, auditable workflow.

## Positioning

FresiCMT's strongest differentiator is one coherent workflow spanning submission through registration, with role-aware experiences and conference-scoped controls throughout.

## Operating Context

The product supports organizers, authors, and reviewers working across calls for papers, manuscript metadata and PDFs, conflict-of-interest declarations, reviewer assignments, structured reviews, decisions, notifications, camera-ready files, and registrations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Conference-scoped roles and role-aware workspaces
- Open, single-blind, and double-blind peer-review configuration
- Conflict-of-interest declarations and assignment safeguards
- Submission, review, decision, camera-ready, and registration workflows
- Responsive web application built with Next.js
- Public claims must remain limited to implemented product workflows; do not invent customers, testimonials, adoption metrics, pricing, or performance benchmarks

## Brand Commitments

The product name is FresiCMT. The voice should be precise, credible, and useful to academic organizers without hype.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains working product surfaces and architecture documentation that can support truthful interface demonstrations. There are currently no approved customer logos, testimonials, adoption metrics, or external proof assets.

## Product Principles

- Make the full conference lifecycle legible as one connected system.
- Earn organizer trust through precision, clarity, and visible controls.
- Show working mechanisms instead of relying on unsupported claims.
- Keep every participant's next action clear within their role.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public experience must be responsive, keyboard accessible, semantically structured, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
