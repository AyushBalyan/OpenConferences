# FresiCMT Design System

## Direction

The visual world is the **Conference Control Sheet**: academic program schedules, peer-review rubrics, editorial production boards, and scientific data graphics translated into a precise web system. The connected conference lifecycle is the primary visual motif.

## Color

- Mineral canvas: `#f4f4f5`
- Paper surface: `#ffffff`
- Deep ink: `#09090b`
- Structural gray: `#52525b`
- Primary blue: `#1d4ed8`
- Signal chartreuse: `#84cc16`, reserved for active, verified, or completed states
- Hairlines use ink or zinc with sufficient contrast; decorative gradients and glass effects are not part of this system.

## Typography

- Headlines: Barlow Condensed, heavy and tightly composed
- Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible, optimized for long operational copy
- Data and identifiers: IBM Plex Sans where compact labels or measurements need distinction
- Display copy remains concise; body measures stay readable and bounded.

## Shape and Structure

- Square and lightly rounded geometry (`2–4px`)
- Ruled lines, registration marks, tabular alignment, status chips, paper identifiers, and stage connectors
- Dense interface demonstrations may use crisp offset shadows; ordinary content relies on structure rather than card shadows
- Cards are not the page scaffold. Sections use matrices, timelines, ledgers, and continuous tracks.

## Interaction

- Focus states are clearly visible in blue with adequate offset.
- Motion is restrained to one lifecycle handoff sequence and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Controls retain explicit labels and real destinations.

## Content Rules

- Demonstration data is labeled illustrative.
- Never fabricate customers, testimonials, adoption numbers, pricing, benchmarks, certifications, or awards.
- Product proof comes from visible workflows, controls, roles, and safeguards.
