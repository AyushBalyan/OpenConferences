# Screen inventory

Every screen and screen-like state the product has today: who uses it, for what task, how it is reached, how much it is used, and what should happen to it in the redesign. This is the Phase 1 input for the navigation work in Phase 3 and the build order in Phase 4.

- **Code read:** `apps/web` and `apps/mobile` on the `redesign` branch, 2026-11-28. `apps/web-v2` is new and not listed.
- **Page views:** the owner's Umami export, totalled per screen with `infra/redesign/audit/umami-by-screen.mjs`.
- **Action counts:** the backup of 2026-11-25, restored locally (decision D11), with `infra/redesign/audit/usage.sql`. The audit log runs from 25 July to 25 November 2026.

## How to read the "Use" column

- **Views and visitors** come from Umami. The rating is by views: very high (1,000 or more), high (200 to 999), medium (50 to 199), low (under 50), not seen.
- **Action counts** come from the audit log in the backup and say how often the task was completed.
- **"Part of its page"** means a tab, step or dialog. Umami cannot tell these apart from the page they sit in.

Limits of the page-view numbers:

- The export holds exactly 500 addresses, which looks like a cap. Individual paper and review pages beyond the cap are missing, so those two screens are undercounted.
- The export does not say what period it covers.
- Sign-in, sign-up, joining and invitation pages are no longer tracked at all (`apps/web/src/app/layout.tsx:71`). Their numbers come from before that rule and are too low.
- "Not seen" can therefore mean unused, untracked, or used before the period.
- There is no role in the data. Role splits come from the backup.

## Needs your decision

1. **Reviewer assignment is spread over five screens** that show the same assignments: the ledger (S-083), the reviewer overview (S-087), Current assignments (S-095), Reviewer bids (S-097) and Manual assignment (S-101). Reminding, extending, releasing and replacing each exist twice with different wording. **Proposed:** merge Current assignments into the ledger, keep Manual assignment as the one "assign" action, and cut Reviewer bids.
2. **About ten screens serve features nobody uses:** bidding oversight (S-075), Reviewer bids and the four conflict pages. Production holds one bid and no conflict declaration. **Proposed:** not built in the first version of `apps/web-v2`; they return as optional modules if an organiser asks.
3. **The chair role.** Nobody holds it, and the menu offers chairs two things the server refuses (invitations and the audit log). **Proposed:** keep the role, and settle what a chair may do in the Phase 3 navigation work.
4. **There are two ways to accept a reviewer invitation** (S-017 and S-021), plus three helper pages. **Proposed:** one acceptance flow with its own failure state.
5. **Carry over is proposed beyond the list in D6** for the privacy and terms text, the invoice PDF and the two CSV exports. The mobile app is marked carry over because it was redesigned in November and sits outside the web work. Say if any of these should be redesigned instead.
6. **Whole screens are missing.** There is no public conference page (the submit link shows a spinner and never names the conference), no profile or security screen (email codes can be switched on but never off), no organisation screens and no platform admin screens. Granting a role needs a raw user ID (S-132). **Proposed:** profile, security and a members list with search join the Phase 4 scope; the public page and admin screens stay in Phase 5.
7. **The paper page gets the most design attention.** It is the busiest screen, the place most emails link to, and the least tidy: six sections that always show, even when empty. The plan's reference flow, an author submitting a paper, ends on it.
8. **Tests are counted in the live statistics.** A fix belongs on `main`, outside the redesign.
